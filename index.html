Research Scientist at FAIR (Meta)

Moab Arar

I work on generative models that learn to create, simulate, and control visual worlds, with a focus on Generative Video Models, Real-time Video Generation, World Models, and personalization of text-to-video and text-to-image models.

I completed my PhD at Tel Aviv University, advised by Amit Bermano and Ariel Shamir. Previously, I was a research intern at Google and Meta.
